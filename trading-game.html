Informed Trading Game

A simple trading simulation in the spirit of Glosten and Milgrom (1985)
Under construction. The game is still being tested and may likely break.
Brian Roseman Research Teaching CV Trading Game Enter the game

A stck is worth $100 or $120, picked at random. The starting fair price is $110.

Students are assigned one of three roles:

  • Market makers post bid and ask quotes. Everyone else trades against them.
  • Informed traders know the final price.
  • Noise traders know only that it is 50/50.

Prices move as market makers read the order flow. The game is zero-sum.

Joining

  1. Open the game on a laptop or phone.
  2. Enter your first name and the four-letter room code.
  3. You're assigned a role when the round starts. Keep it to yourself.
  4. If you lose connection, reopen the link. Your role and position are restored.

Roles

Market maker

  • Always shows a bid and an ask, at most $6 apart.
  • Buys at the bid and sells at the ask. Other traders take those prices as given.
  • Profit is based on earning the spread.
  • Identity is public.
Practice as a market maker

Informed trader

  • Knows the true value from the beginning.
  • Profits by trading toward it without letting others know.
  • Identity is private.
Practice as an informed trader

Noise trader

  • Knows only the 50/50 odds.
  • Must still trade on most turns.
  • Learns from other's trades.
Practice as a noise trader

Rules

Traders

  • Trade in order for a set number of rounds.
  • On your turn: buy or sell 1 to 3 shares, or play a pass card.
  • Buy at the best ask, sells at the best bid. If both market makers show the same price, the trader chooses.
  • No negotiation. The whole order fills at the quoted price.
  • Short positions are allowed.

Market makers

  • Post an opening quote before the first turn.
  • Always post a quote.
  • Quotes are frozen while a trader decides.
  • After each trade there is a short quote window.
  • Quotes may be changed at any time, with changes landing after the current trader.

Instructors and Hosts

  • One person hosts and does not trade. Choose "Start a game" to get a room code.
  • The host screen deals roles, runs turns, shows every position, and exports the trade log.
  • Settings can be customized.
