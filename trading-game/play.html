How the game works

A stock is worth either $100 or $120, decided by a coin flip, so the fair starting price is $110. The game is zero-sum.

  • Market makers (usually two) always show a bid and an ask no more than $6 apart and take the other side of every trade.
  • Informed traders are told the true value on their role card. Noise traders know only the 50/50 odds. Nobody but the host knows who is informed.
  • Traders go one at a time in a fixed order for a set number of laps. On your turn you buy or sell 1 to 3 shares at the best quote, or play a pass card. With no pass cards left you must trade.
  • Quotes freeze during a trader's turn. After each trade there is a short quote window, then market makers' pending quotes go live and the next trader is up.
  • Because the true value is unknown, P&L is shown in two columns: what you would make if the stock is $100 and if it is $120. At the reveal everyone is ranked within their role.
